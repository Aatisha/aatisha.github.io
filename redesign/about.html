About me

I believe that most of my work is a kind of translation: taking something tangled and making it clear to the person who has to live with it.

I build things, and I break them. Not on purpose, of course. But I’ve come to trust the breaking. It’s where a design tells me what it actually is, rather than what I hoped it would be.

I’m a product designer at LifeLoop, where I design experiences for senior living communities, for the staff who keep those places running and the residents who call them home. Lately, much of that work has involved AI, and it has taught me that the best technology begins with paying close attention to people.
